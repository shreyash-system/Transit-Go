The Problem We're Solving

Every Pune commuter knows this frustration.

😠

No Live Tracking

You have no idea where the bus actually is. You just wait and wait and wait.

🕐

Unpredictable Delays

Bus says 9:15 AM. It comes at 9:47 AM. No updates. No alerts. Just missed class.

😨

Overcrowded Buses

You squeeze in hoping for space. No way to know if the next bus will be less crowded.

💸

Paper Tickets and Cash

Standing in line for tokens. Carrying exact change. It is 2025. Why is this still a thing?

How TransitGo Works

Built from real user research.

Every feature comes directly from interviews and empathy mapping of real PMPML commuters in Pune.

01

Search Your Route

Enter your start and destination. Get all bus options with live timing, fare, and route info in seconds.

02

Track in Real-Time

See exactly where your bus is on a live map. Get a notification when it is 2 stops away so you are never late.

03

Book and Pay Digitally

Reserve your seat and pay with UPI or card. No tokens, no cash, no queues. Just scan and board.

04

Get Smart Alerts

Instant notifications for delays, cancellations, and route changes. Know before you leave home.
