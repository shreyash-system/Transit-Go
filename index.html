The Problem We Solved

Every Pune commuter knows this frustration.

Born from direct commuter interviews and empathy mapping at Swargate, Katraj, and Deccan bus stops.

😠

No Live Tracking

Commuters stood at stops with zero info on where their bus was or when it would reach.

⏱️

Unpredictable Delays

Buses scheduled for 9:15 AM arriving at 9:45 AM with no warning, causing missed lectures and shifts.

👥

Severe Overcrowding

No way to know passenger loads in advance, forcing riders into packed buses instead of waiting 5 min for an empty one.

💸

Cash & Paper Inconvenience

Carrying exact coins, standing in token lines, and losing paper slips in 2025.

How TransitGo Works

Unified transit in 4 simple steps.

A seamless companion built for daily commuters, students, and professionals across Pune.

01

Search Any Route

Enter your origin and destination. Get intelligent bus and Pune Metro options with calculated fares, intermediate stops, and total duration.

02

Live GPS & Metro Map

Watch moving buses and metro trains live on an interactive Leaflet map with pulsing radar beacons, line filters, and accurate ETAs.

03

Instant UPI E-Tickets

Book digital tickets in 3 steps, pay via UPI or card, and generate an interactive 3D boarding pass with scannable QR security code.

04

Disruption & Crowd Alerts

Stay ahead with real-time delay warnings, road repair alerts, and 24-hour AI crowd forecasts modeled on Pune rush hours.
